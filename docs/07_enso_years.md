# ENSO years for the La Niña vs El Niño contrast (Q2)

Basis: NOAA CPC **Oceanic Niño Index (ONI)** — 3-month running mean of the Niño-3.4 SST anomaly; an event needs
|ONI| ≥ 0.5 °C over 5 overlapping seasons; strength = weak / moderate / strong / very strong.

## Classification of the candidate years

- **2010–2011 — STRONG La Niña** (one of the strongest of the recent record). → **2011 is a solid La Niña year**,
  and it drove the catastrophic 2010–2011 floods in the lower Magdalena (Mojana/Mompós).
- **2015–2016 — VERY STRONG ("super") El Niño** (top-3 all-time, with 1982–83 and 1997–98). → the clearest El Niño
  of the period.
- **2016–17 and 2017–18 — WEAK La Niña / neutral.** → **2017 is NOT an El Niño year**; it was weak La Niña/neutral.

## Recommendation

- **La Niña year: 2011** (from the strong 2010–2011 event).
- **El Niño year: 2015–2016** (very strong).
- **Drop 2017** as the El Niño candidate — it was actually (weak) La Niña; using it would blur the contrast.
- 2011 (strong cold phase) vs 2015–2016 (very strong warm phase) gives the **cleanest, strongest opposite-phase
  contrast** in the recent record — ideal for the study, and both overlap the IDEAM records (discharge certain;
  sediment coverage to confirm on DHIME, see `06_ideam_stations.md`).
